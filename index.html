Research

A Partial-likelihood Approach for the Analysis of Discrete-Time Competing Risks Data
Seohyun Lee, Minjung Lee, and Sangbum Choi

Proposes a Poisson-approximation-based partial likelihood for cause-specific regression with grouped event times, together with a Breslow-type baseline hazard estimator and a cluster-robust sandwich variance. A higher-order trend-filtering penalty on the baseline hazards, solved via ADMM, stabilizes estimation, and the method is evaluated through simulation studies and an application to colon cancer data from the SEER registry (N = 9,104).
